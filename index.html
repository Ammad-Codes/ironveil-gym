The Club

A sanctuary for those who train with intent

IRONVEIL is not a gym that happens to be expensive. It is a deliberately small club, designed around one idea: when the environment is exacting, the person inside it rises to meet it.

From the hand-finished platforms to the whisper-quiet recovery suites, every detail exists to remove friction between you and the work. Our coaches are sport scientists, former athletes and obsessive craftspeople. Our members are founders, surgeons, artists and athletes who value discretion as much as results.

  • Capped membership

    Never crowded. Never a queue for a rack.

  • Performance lab

    Biometrics, VO₂ testing and quarterly reviews.

  • Recovery atelier

    Cold plunge, thermal suite and guided mobility.

  • Concierge service

    Towels, nutrition and bookings handled for you.
